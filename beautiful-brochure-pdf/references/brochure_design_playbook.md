# Brochure Design Playbook

Use this reference when a brochure needs art direction, page planning, or a stronger print-design structure.

## Default Formats

| Format | Use for | Default structure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A4 portrait, 6 pages | Compact product/service brochure | Cover, problem/value, features, proof, process, contact |
| A4 portrait, 8 pages | Company profile or richer service brochure | Cover, intro, differentiators, service/product pages, case/proof, process, contact |
| A5 landscape, 8-12 pages | Premium editorial booklet | Cover, editorial opener, image-led spreads, detail pages, closing |
| Tri-fold | Event handout, simple local service, quick sales sheet | Cover, promise, 3 selling points, proof, contact |

## Page Archetypes

- Cover: brand/product signal, short promise, strong visual, minimal copy.
- Editorial opener: one large statement, concise supporting paragraph, atmospheric but relevant visual.
- Feature grid: 3-5 advantages with icons, proof points, or small product images.
- Product/service detail: practical specs, workflow, scope, deliverables, or package tiers.
- Proof page: customer story, metrics, before/after, certification, partner logos, or testimonial.
- Process page: timeline, steps, onboarding path, implementation flow, or service journey.
- Comparison page: old vs new, plan tiers, package options, feature matrix.
- Closing page: contact, QR/code placeholder, address, CTA, and brand signoff.

## Style Directions

### Refined Luxury

Use generous margins, quiet color, high-contrast typography, large photography, thin rules, and restrained metallic or deep accent colors. Avoid dense grids.

### Technical Precision

Use modular grids, clear diagrams, crisp iconography, neutral base colors, one sharp accent, and structured specifications. Avoid decorative fluff.

### Warm Service

Use human photography, approachable spacing, softer accents, rounded but not pill-like forms, and reassuring proof. Avoid stocky smiles and vague claims.

### Editorial Magazine

Use strong display type, asymmetry, pull quotes, full-bleed images, section openers, and varied pacing. Keep body copy disciplined.

### Financial Trust

Use calm contrast, clean tables, conservative typography, confidence-building metrics, and clear compliance/disclaimer placement. Avoid flashy gradients.

### Education Friendly

Use clear sequence, approachable diagrams, ample white space, warm secondary colors, and benefit-led headings. Avoid childish decoration unless the audience is children.

## Copy Compression

When source copy is long:

- Convert repeated claims into one positioning sentence.
- Turn features into benefit/proof pairs.
- Move implementation detail into a process or specification page.
- Keep paragraphs short enough to scan in print.
- Use captions to make images carry meaning, not decoration.

## Visual QA Checklist

- Cover immediately communicates what is being promoted.
- No page has more than one competing hero headline.
- Text blocks align to a consistent grid.
- Images are not blurry, stretched, or awkwardly cropped.
- Page numbers and footer marks are consistent.
- Chinese punctuation and line breaks look natural.
- Contact/CTA page is complete and easy to scan.
- PDF render matches the HTML preview closely.
